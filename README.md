# The Office

**Genre:** Comedy / Mockumentary  
**Setting:** Dunder Mifflin Paper Company, Scranton, PA  

> Everyday work life has never been this awkward — or this funny.

---

## About  
A documentary crew follows the employees of Dunder Mifflin as they navigate office drama, romance, and ridiculous antics under their eccentric boss, **Michael Scott**.

---

### Key Characters
- Michael Scott  
- Jim Halpert  
- Pam Beesly  
- Dwight Schrute
